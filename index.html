ABOUT ME

I am a Data Science undergrad at United International University, but my real classroom is the space between a complex algorithm and a human being. While the theoretical math of machine learning forms my foundation, my obsession lies in the engineering. I am driven by the thrill of taking a raw, abstract model and forging it into a functional tool that someone can actually use.

I don't believe in building AI in a vacuum. Technology should serve the community it belongs to. By focusing my projects on regional, real-world problems, I’ve seen firsthand how raw data—when structured with purpose—can directly improve daily life. Whether I’m engineering a new system or applying data science to active business environments, I no longer see just numbers on a screen; I see the pulse of the market and the actionable insights that drive real decisions.

However, I am very much an "Aspirant." I am still actively wrestling with the heavy calculus of backpropagation and the deep mechanics of neural networks. Right now, I am pushing hard past my comfort zone—bridging the gap between training static models and mastering the backend architectures required to deploy robust, end-to-end AI pipelines.

> I am learning. I am building. And I am always hunting for the next problem to solve.
